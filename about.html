About Us

Our Story, Our Mission, Our Promise to Every Family

Al Quranul Academy exists to make authentic, correct Quran education accessible — wherever in the world you call home.

Our Story

Built for Families Living Far From Home

Al Quranul Academy began with a simple observation: Bangladeshi and Muslim families living abroad often struggle to find Quran teachers who combine real qualification, patience with children, and scheduling that respects busy, cross-timezone family life.

We set out to build an academy where every class is one-to-one, every teacher is carefully selected, and every family — whether in Dhaka or Doha, Paris or Perth — can access the same standard of authentic Quran education.

Our Mission

To make authentic Quran education accessible for children and adults through flexible, one-to-one online classes — regardless of where in the world a family lives.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted online Quran academies for Bangladeshi and international Muslim families, known for the quality of our teachers and the care behind every class.

Our Teaching Method

Structured Progress, Personal Pace

Every course follows a clear, proven structure — but every class is shaped around the individual student in front of the teacher.

One Student, One Teacher

No shared classrooms — full attention on your pronunciation, pace, and questions, every single session.

Tracked, Reported Progress

Teachers log progress after every class, so parents always know exactly where their child stands.

Correction Before Speed

We prioritise correct pronunciation and Tajweed over rushing through material.
