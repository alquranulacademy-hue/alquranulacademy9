About the Academy

Authentic Quran Education, Wherever You Call Home

Al Quranul Academy was founded to make correct, authentic Quran education accessible to Bangladeshi and Muslim families living abroad — from Dhaka to Doha, London to Los Angeles. We believe every student, young or old, deserves a patient teacher, a personal pace, and a class time that fits real family life.

Our teaching philosophy is simple: one student, one teacher, one clear path of progress — built on correct pronunciation, real understanding, and genuine care for every learner's journey through the Quran.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can adults join the classes?+

Yes — we welcome learners of every age. Many of our students are adults learning to read the Quran for the first time or refining their Tajweed.

Can I choose a male or female teacher?+

Absolutely. You can request a male or female teacher for your child or yourself when you book your free trial or enrol.

How do one-to-one classes work online?+

Each class is a live, private video session between your teacher and student — no shared classrooms, so every minute is focused on your progress.

Is the free trial really free?+

Yes, completely free with no obligation. It is simply a chance to meet a teacher and experience our teaching style before you decide to enrol.
